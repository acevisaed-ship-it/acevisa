Study Visa Services

A Service Policy is read together with the General Terms of Service. If they differ, this Service Policy applies to this service.

A1  Scope. The Services may include: counselling on countries, institutions and programmes; shortlisting; preparing and submitting admission and scholarship applications; document checklists; coordinating attestation and translation; guidance on personal statements and CVs; language-test guidance; financial-document guidance; visa application preparation; appointment assistance; and interview preparation. Only the items listed on the invoice are provided.
A2  No guarantee. The Company does not guarantee admission, an offer letter, a scholarship, a visa, post-study work rights, residence or employment.
A3  Institution decisions and fees. Application fees, tuition deposits and accommodation deposits are paid to institutions and are governed by each institution's own refund policy. The Company does not control them and does not guarantee any refund from an institution.
A4  Eligibility information. Entry rules on grades, language tests, study gaps and age differ by institution and change over time. What the Company says about them is general guidance only (see clause 4.3).
A5  Financial evidence. Bank statements, blocked-account proofs, sponsorship letters and other financial documents must be genuine and based on the Client's own funds or a genuine sponsor. The Company will not arrange temporary, borrowed or "show" funds to make an account look larger.
A6  Stages. The Services run in stages (documents, admission and offer, university requirements, visa filing, outcome). Instalments follow the stages shown on the invoice.
A7  Refusal. If a visa or admission is refused, clause 7 applies. A fresh application may need new fees. [Company to confirm.]
A8  After the outcome. No service is provided after the visa or admission outcome, unless listed on the invoice.
