Other Visas, Residence and Document-Preparation Services

A Service Policy is read together with the General Terms of Service. If they differ, this Service Policy applies to this service.

This policy covers work-related and residence visas, income-based residence visas (such as D7), attestation, translation, letters, appointment assistance and ad-hoc services recommended by a counselor.

C1  Scope. Only document preparation, application support, forms, checklists, appointment assistance and coordination of attestation and translation, as listed on the invoice.
C2  No employment services. The Company does not find, offer, arrange or guarantee jobs, employers or sponsors, and does not take money for any job. A work-related visa needs a genuine job offer or contract that the Client obtains themselves.
C3  Residence and income-based visas. Eligibility depends on the Client's own genuine income, funds, accommodation and the destination's rules. The Company will not arrange show funds. The Company is not a law firm or tax adviser and gives no legal or tax advice. It may suggest a licensed professional, whose fees the Client pays.
C4  Letters and certificates. The Company prepares letters (for example cover, employment, experience or sponsorship letters) only where they truthfully reflect facts the Client has confirmed and, where relevant, where the genuine issuer signs or issues them. The Company never produces an experience or employment letter for a job that was not held.
C5  Attestation, translation and courier. These are carried out by third parties. Times are outside the Company's control and their fees are extra.
C6  Extra services. Services recommended by a counselor and accepted by the Client are confirmed on an invoice (clause 5.5) and are subject to the same no-guarantee and lawful-use rules.
C7  New categories. The Company may add further visa categories. The General Terms of Service apply to them from the date they are added to the invoice.
