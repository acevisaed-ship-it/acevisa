A Service Policy is read together with the General Terms of Service. If they differ, this Service Policy applies to this service.

B1  Scope. The Services may include: assessment of the Client's profile and documents; document checklist; form filling; cover-letter drafting; appointment assistance; travel-insurance guidance; itinerary and accommodation planning guidance; and interview preparation. Only the items listed on the invoice are provided.
B2  Purpose. The Client confirms the purpose is a genuine short visit (for example to family or friends, tourism or a business visit) to [destination]. The Client has not paid for and has not been promised any job, study, settlement or emigration, and will leave within the permitted period of stay.
B3  Invitations and sponsors. Invitation letters and sponsor documents must be genuine and issued by the sponsor. The Company does not produce or buy them.
B4  Bookings. Flight and hotel bookings or reservations used with an application must reflect real intended travel. The Company does not create fake bookings. The Client bears the cost of any booking and its cancellation terms.
B5  Schengen short stays (where applicable). Under the Schengen Visa Code (Regulation (EC) No 810/2009) a short-stay visa allows a stay of up to 90 days in any 180-day period. It is decided by the authorities of the Schengen state concerned. Travel medical insurance (minimum EUR 30,000) is required. Incorrect statements or documents can lead to refusal, annulment or an entry ban.
B6  Other destinations. Rules for other destinations differ. The Client must check them and the Company will point to the official source.
B7  Appeal. The refusal notice states the body and time limit for an appeal. For example, for a Belgian short-stay refusal an appeal lies with the Council for Alien Law Litigation within 30 days of notification, and the Embassy cannot receive appeals. The Client must give instructions promptly (clause 7).
B8  Entry and departure. A visa does not guarantee entry. Border officers may refuse entry, and FIA Immigration may refuse to allow departure from Pakistan (clause 10.4).
