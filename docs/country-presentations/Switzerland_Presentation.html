Section 1

Country Snapshot

Tuition Range (National)
CHF 435 - 4,000 / semester
Federal institutes (ETH Zurich, EPFL) sit at the top for non-EU (CHF 2,190/semester); USI is highest overall (CHF 4,000). See each institution's own profile for its specific figure.
Language Divide
Bachelor's mostly local language; Master's often English
Bachelor's programmes are predominantly German/French/Italian-taught; Master's programmes at STEM-focused universities (ETH, EPFL, UZH) are almost entirely in English.
Institutions Covered
35 shown (10 fully profiled)
10 cantonal universities + 2 federal institutes of technology + 9 universities of applied sciences + 14 universities of teacher education, nationally — every one of the 35 appears individually in this deck.
Visa Timeline (Pakistan)
At least 8 weeks before entry
Apply for the residence permit as soon as the acceptance letter is received — permit processing takes 4–8 weeks.
Study Gap & Age Policy
No unified federal policy
Each university sets its own rule. Bachelor's typically tolerates 1–2 years, Master's up to 5. Several alternative-admission minimums: University of Bern 30+, University of Geneva and Lausanne 25+ with 3 years' professional experience, USI Lugano 25+ without formal qualifications. General minimum entry age 18.
Application Fee
CHF 65 - 150
Varies by institution — USI waives its CHF 100 fee for Lisbon Convention countries, including Pakistan.
Health Insurance, PR Pathway, Citizenship Pathway, Dual Nationality, Embassy Warnings, National Application Portal
Information required for all of these — none were confirmed in this research pass. Do not state a position on any of these to a client without checking a current, authoritative source first. There is no single national application portal — each institution runs its own admissions portal, listed individually in each institution's profile in Section 3.

• ETH Zurich: HSC plus a certificate of university admission plus a passing score on the Reduced Entrance Examination ETHZ — this exam is the main gatekeeper for Pakistani Bachelor's applicants; budget serious prep time
• IMI Luzern (private, outside the public master list): HSC with at least 60% is sufficient — one of the more accessible pathways in the country
• Foreign qualifications may additionally require the Swiss Federal Baccalaureate Examination, depending on the institution
• English requirement: IELTS 7.0+ / TOEFL iBT 100+ typical at top universities; French B2/C1 for EPFL Bachelor's
Visa & Residence Permit Timeline
Swiss student visa processing: at least 8 weeks prior to entry. Apply for the residence permit as soon as the acceptance letter is received — processing takes 4–8 weeks. ETH Zurich (8 weeks) and EPFL (8–12 weeks) have the most clearly documented processing times; University of Geneva has the fastest (up to 3 weeks).
Study Gap & Age Policy (Country-Wide)
No unified federal gap-year policy — each university sets its own rule. Bachelor's typically tolerates 1–2 years, Master's up to 5 years. No universal age ceiling, but several alternative-admission routes carry their own minimums: University of Bern 30+, University of Geneva and Lausanne 25+ with 3 years' professional experience, USI Lugano 25+ without formal qualifications. General minimum entry age is 18.
Counselor Note
Switzerland's real differentiator for Pakistani clients is its top-ranked STEM Master's programmes at ETH Zurich and EPFL, both taught almost entirely in English — but ETH's Reduced Entrance Exam is a genuine, serious gatekeeper for undergraduate applicants and should be flagged early. Universities of Teacher Education are essentially not viable for international English-taught degree-seekers; do not present them as such.
