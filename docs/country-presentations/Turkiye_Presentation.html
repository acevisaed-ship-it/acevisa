Section 1

Country Snapshot

Standout -- Exceptionally Low Public Tuition
EUR 250-700/year at public universities -- among the cheapest in Europe
Master's: EUR 500-1,200/year. This is a genuine differentiator versus every other country in this dataset -- worth leading with for cost-sensitive clients. Private/foundation universities charge far more ($4,000-20,000+/year; elite institutions like Koc run up to $59,000 for Medicine). This is a country-wide band, not a guarantee for any specific institution -- always confirm the exact per-institution, per-programme figure in Section 5.
TR-YÖS Exam
Mandatory at most public universities
Minimum 300 points, valid 2 years, administered twice yearly by OSYM (TR-YOS/1: 12 April 2027; TR-YOS/2: 11 October 2027). SAT/IB/GCE A-Level/ABITUR accepted as alternatives at some institutions -- confirm per institution.
Türkiye Bursları Scholarship
Full funding, ~50 spots for Pakistan
Covers tuition + stipend + accommodation + health insurance + 1-year Turkish course. IELTS/TOEFL not mandatory. Apply 10 Jan - 20 Feb 2027 -- highly competitive (~250,000 applicants for ~5,000 global spots).

Türkiye has 208 universities nationally (129 public + 79 private) -- the source's own registry is an explicit curated "Top Universities" subset, not a claim to cover all 208. Every institution named anywhere in the source (registry, programme data, eligibility, fees, deadlines, scholarships, or extension history) appears here individually.
Visa Financial Requirement
PKR 2,000,000 - 3,500,000
~$2,200-5,500 -- processing ~15 working days via Anatolia Travel Services (Islamabad, Karachi, Lahore, Peshawar + more).
Scale
208 universities nationally
129 public + 79 private · 362,000+ international students in 2025 · 26 Turkish universities in QS World Rankings 2026 (top: METU 269th, ITU 298th, Koc 323rd, Bogazici 371st, Sabanci 404th, Bilkent 415th).
Document Attestation
IBCC + HEC + MoFA required
School certificates via IBCC, degrees via HEC, all documents via Pakistan's Ministry of Foreign Affairs, plus Apostille and certified Turkish/English translations.
Health Insurance, PR Pathway, Citizenship Pathway, Dual Nationality, National Application Portal
Information required for all of these — none were confirmed in this research pass beyond the requirement that health insurance be arranged before residence-permit application (Section 6B). Do not state a position on PR/citizenship pathways to a client without checking a current, authoritative source first. There is no single national application portal — each institution runs its own admissions portal, listed individually in each institution's profile in Section 3.

Pakistan-Specific Requirements
• TR-YOS mandatory at most (not all) public universities -- register through OSYM; minimum 300 points; valid 2 years; SAT/IB/GCE A-Levels/ABITUR/TQDK accepted as alternatives at some institutions.
• UG general requirement: High school diploma (FSc/HSSC/A-Levels/IB) with minimum GPA 2.5/4.0; IELTS 5.5-6.0 for English-taught programmes.

Counselor Note
