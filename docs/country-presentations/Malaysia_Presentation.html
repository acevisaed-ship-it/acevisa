Section 1

Country Snapshot

Pakistan-Specific Admission Advantage
Matric/FA/FSc are explicitly confirmed accepted for undergraduate admission
Pakistani qualifications (Matriculation, FA, FSc) are named directly alongside A-Levels, IB, and Malaysia's own STPM/UEC as accepted UG entry routes -- a genuinely favourable, explicitly documented fit. EMGS (Education Malaysia Global Services) visa processing is confirmed required for all international students. One institution-specific caveat: UKM (Universiti Kebangsaan Malaysia) only accepts A-Level grades A to D -- always confirm current grade-acceptance policy per institution.
Tuition (International)
RM 8,000 - 67,000/year
Public universities: RM 8,000-18,000/year; private/foreign branch campuses: RM 25,000-67,000/year -- a wide range, always quote per-institution.
Application Deadline
~1 July 2026 (UPM international)
Varies considerably by institution -- most public universities run a September-intake window opening in early August 2026; see each institution's own profile in Section 3.
Institutions Covered
41 shown (8 fully profiled)
20 public (IPTA) + 12 private (IPTS) + 9 foreign branch campuses, exactly matching the source's own stated master-list total of 41. The source's own Data Quality Report discloses full research for only a subset -- the remaining institutions appear as registry-only entries rather than omitted.
Language Requirement
IELTS 5.5-6.5 / TOEFL iBT 70-90
Varies by programme; higher requirements typically apply for Medicine, Law, and Engineering.
Visa Process
EMGS processing required
Education Malaysia Global Services (EMGS) handles the national student-visa approval process for all international students -- confirmed required, but turnaround time was not confirmed in this research pass.
Full English-Medium Instruction
Confirmed at Universiti Malaya
UM specifically confirms all undergraduate and postgraduate programmes are taught in English with no Malay-language requirement for international students -- a genuine differentiator worth highlighting to a client wary of a Malay-language requirement.
Health Insurance, PR Pathway, Citizenship Pathway, Dual Nationality, Embassy Warnings, National Application Portal, Turnaround Time
Information required for all of these — none were confirmed in this research pass (turnaround time is separately flagged "Not yet extracted" in the source's own Data Quality Report). Do not state a position on any of these to a client without checking a current, authoritative source first. There is no single national application portal — each institution runs its own admissions portal, listed individually in each institution's profile in Section 3.
