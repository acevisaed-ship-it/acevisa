Pakistan-Specific Deadline Alert
Riga Technical University (RTU): early deadline 1 May 2026 for Pakistani applicants
Grouped with ~20 other countries, this is significantly earlier than RTU's general non-EU window of 15 June–15 July 2026. This applies to RTU's main campus and its Academy of Liepaja (they share deadlines). Other Latvian institutions generally follow the standard non-EU window of 1 May–15 July 2026 (varies by institution, see Section 9).
Tuition Range (National)
EUR 1,500 - 15,000 / year
Widest range driven by Medicine/Dentistry at RSU (up to EUR 15,000); most Bachelor's programmes fall EUR 2,600–5,800/year. See each institution's own profile for its specific figure.
Institutions Covered
12 shown, all 12 fully profiled
11 state universities + Riga Technical University's Academy of Liepaja (its own campus with its own English-taught programmes) — Latvia's own master list has no hidden gap.
Visa Timeline (Pakistan)
Official: 2-4 weeks; RTU: 3-12 weeks
Reddit/Discord student-forum sources cite 4–8 weeks reflecting full processing including visa-related steps — treat as an estimate, not a guarantee.
Health Insurance, PR Pathway, Citizenship Pathway, Dual Nationality, Embassy Warnings, National Application Portal, Study Gap & Age Policy
Information required for all of these — none were confirmed in this research pass. Do not state a position on any of these to a client without checking a current, authoritative source first. There is no single national application portal — each institution runs its own admissions portal, listed individually in each institution's profile in Section 3.
