Coverage Note -- Read Before Using This Deck
This deck is built from general visa/counseling guidance, not institution-level research
Unlike every other country deck in this set, the New Zealand source material is a country-wide counseling guide (intakes, eligibility bands, tuition/living-cost RANGES, visa process) rather than per-institution profiles. No institution in the source reports its own individual tuition fee, application deadline, or specific programme list. This is a confirmed, genuine limitation of the source document, not a research gap that further extraction would close -- see Section 11 for full detail. The 13 institutions below (8 universities + 5 institutes of technology) are shown with verified official websites (researched separately, live web search Sept 2026) and their source-given programme AREAS (subject strengths, not individual programme names); the 7 private colleges are named only, with no further detail in source. Always confirm exact fees, deadlines, and specific programmes on each institution's own site before quoting a client.

Section 7

Visa & Post-Study Work

Work Rights While Studying
Up to 25 hours/week during studies, full-time during scheduled breaks where permitted. Common sectors: retail, hospitality, warehousing, delivery, customer service, administration.
Post-Study Work Visa (PSW)
Many graduates become eligible, typically for up to 3 years depending on qualification and study level. Allows working for any employer, gaining NZ experience, and building toward residence.
Per separate practical-info research: PSW eligibility is set to extend to Graduate Diploma at NZQCF Level 7 from 16 November 2026 -- flag this upcoming change to clients planning study beyond that date.
Permanent Residency Pathways
Study → Post-study work visa → Skilled employment → Residence application. Many graduates pursue residence after securing skilled employment.
Dependents
Spouse may be eligible for a work visa depending on the student's qualification. Dependent children may accompany eligible students, with additional financial evidence required.
Visa Interviews & Document Attestation
Generally no university interview and no embassy interview for most institutions, though Immigration New Zealand may request additional information. Attestation: Intermediate documents — Board + IBCC + MOFA; Bachelor's documents — HEC + MOFA.

Why Institution-Level Fields Are Mostly Information required Here
This is a genuine, confirmed source limitation, not an extraction gap. New Zealand's source document (a 20-section counseling guide covering intakes, eligibility, financials, visa process, and PR pathways) is structured entirely around country-wide guidance -- it never reports an individual institution's tuition fee, application deadline, admissions-portal URL, or specific programme name (only broad "programme areas" / subject strengths for the 13 named universities and ITPs). Every other country deck in this set is built from per-institution research documents that do contain this level of detail; New Zealand's source simply does not. Re-running extraction on this source will not surface data that was never collected -- closing this gap would require new institution-level research, not better parsing.
