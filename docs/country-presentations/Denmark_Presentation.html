Study Gap & Age Policy
No age barrier by law
Danish law explicitly prohibits universities from using age as a selection criterion. Gap years are broadly accepted — Bachelor's typically up to 2–6 years, Master's 5–8 years with relevant experience. Minimum entry age 18 (by 1 June of the application year, non-EU/EEA).
Visa Timeline (Pakistan)
Approx. 4–8 weeks total
Danish Embassy Islamabad: ~15 days · Student residence permit (SIRI): 1–2 months, up to 3 if more information is requested.
Application Fee
None (national)

Study Gap & Age Policy (Country-Wide)
Danish law explicitly prohibits universities from using age as a selection criterion. Gap years are broadly accepted — Bachelor's typically up to 2–6 years, Master's 5–8 years with relevant experience. No upper age limit; minimum entry age 18 (by 1 June of the application year for non-EU/EEA citizens).
Counselor Note
Unlike Germany, Denmark charges real tuition to non-EU students at every public institution — do not present Denmark as a low-cost or tuition-free option. Confirm the exact figure per programme before quoting, since the ranges vary significantly by subject and institution. Denmark's real strength for Pakistani clients is its explicit legal ban on age discrimination and its broad gap-year acceptance — a genuine differentiator worth leading with for atypical applicant profiles.
