Study Gap & Age Policy
Among the most flexible destinations in this dataset — study gaps and age are explicitly described as "no barrier". Undergraduate gaps of 2–5 years are fine, postgraduate gaps are widely accepted, and a justified gap of up to 10 years (some institutions up to 15) can be considered. No hard age ceiling; 18–25 preferred for undergraduate, 21–40 preferred for postgraduate, but exceptions are routine.
Rolling Admissions Windows (Private Universities)
• European University Cyprus: January–August
• UCLan Cyprus: October–July (course start October–May)
• Frederick University: January–August (fee note: rolling per source, some documents cite up to 31 July)
• Neapolis University Pafos: rolling for most programmes; doctoral programmes 15 July–30 August 2026
• University of Nicosia: rolling, January–August, with a defined October Fall-intake date
Counselor Note
Cyprus is a strong option for clients with an atypical academic history (a long study gap, an older applicant, a lower GPA than European averages) — its flexibility is a genuine differentiator, not just a marketing line, but always pair it with a clear fee and deadline confirmation per institution.
