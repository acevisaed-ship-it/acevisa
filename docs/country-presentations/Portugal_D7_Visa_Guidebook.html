Section 4

What the D7 visa actually is

D7 is Portugal’s national residence visa for people who can live from their own regular income earned outside Portugal. It is a relocation visa, not a travel document.

Residence visa for establishment of residence for pensioners, religious purposes, or people living on their own income.

Typical qualifying streams: pensions, rent, dividends, interest, fund distributions, royalties / intellectual property, and other documented own-income that does not depend on a Portuguese job.

D7 is

A two-stage residence route. First a visa sticker in Pakistan. Then a plastic residence card from AIMA in Portugal. After the card, you generally live, study, use public healthcare, travel Schengen 90/180, and may work.

D7 is not

A Schengen tourist visa · a job-seeker visa · the digital nomad visa (D8) · Golden Visa / ARI · a way to stay in Pakistan and only visit Europe a few weeks a year.

The two-stage structure

StageWhat you getWhereValidity
1. National D7 visaStamp/sticker allowing entry to apply for residenceIslamabad embassy via Gerry’s4 months, usually 2 entries
2. Residence permitAutorização de residência — you become a residentAIMA in Portugal (ex-SEF)First card 2 years, then 3 years
Lawful route from Pakistan

Apply from Pakistan → enter on the D7 visa → finish the AIMA appointment in Portugal. You cannot normally switch from tourist status to D7 inside the country.

Section 5

Is D7 the right visa?

Answer this before NIF fees, leases, or SWIFT transfers. Mixing D7 and D8 in one file is a classic delay.

Good fit if you

Are 18+ with a Pakistani (or other non-EU) passport · have stable documented income that is not a 9-to-5 in Portugal · will actually live in Portugal most of the year · have a clean record · can apostille documents at MOFA · can hold euros in Portugal.

Bad fit if you

Only have salary / freelance / remote-job income (use D8) · want to remain in Pakistan/Dubai/UK and visit Europe · have a lump sum but no recurring income · cannot explain source of funds · expect the old NHR tax holiday.

Rule of thumb

Pension + rent + dividends → D7. Remote job for a US/EU/Gulf company → D8. Café / IT company / import business in Portugal → D2. Large capital, no wish to move → Golden Visa if a route still fits. Choose the category that matches the majority of the income.

Section 6

Eligibility A to Z

Personal

  • Non-EU/EEA/Swiss — Pakistani citizens qualify.
  • Main applicant 18+.
  • Intention to establish residence, not a holiday.
  • Apply from the country of legal residence. Live in Pakistan → Islamabad. Live in UAE/UK/Canada on a permit → apply there.

Character

  • Police Character Certificate from Pakistan, and from any country of 12+ months residence.
  • PCC issued less than 90 days before submission, MOFA apostille.
  • Sign the Portuguese criminal-record consultation form.
  • Serious convictions can bar visa and later citizenship.

Health

Travel medical insurance covering necessary treatment, urgent care, and repatriation. After residence: SNS plus, in practice, private cover while registration settles.

Money

Means for at least 12 months on the Portaria 1563/2007 scale. For own-income applicants: prove existence and amount of income from property, IP and/or investments, and prove availability of that income in Portugal (Portuguese bank). Pensioners: pension amount plus availability in Portugal.

Housing — the Islamabad nuance

Checklist vs reality

The Islamabad D7 PDF does not tick a 12-month lease as a separate line, unlike some other consulates. That does not make housing optional. The consulate may still request it. AIMA will require accommodation for the card. Best practice: NIF + Portuguese bank + 12-month registered rental or a deed before you lodge.

What the official Islamabad D7 PDF actually demands

General: signed national form + 2 photos · passport valid 3+ months beyond stay + bio-page copy · proof of regular status if not a national of the filing country · travel insurance · PCC < 90 days apostilled · 12 months’ financial resources.

Own income: documents proving existence and amount of income from movable/immovable property, IP and/or financial investments and availability in national territory.

Pensioners: pension amount + guarantee of receipt / availability in national territory.

Consular discretion

The post may demand extra documents. Signing the “examine as is” declaration with missing papers is a common path to refusal. Incomplete emails for appointments are ignored.

Visa floor ≠ lifestyle

These are legal minima, not a Lisbon budget. A realistic single-person life is closer to €1,500–€2,500/month. Filing at exactly €920 invites both refusal risk and a hard landing.

Income versus savings

  • Law: means for 12 months.
  • D7 category: ongoing own income.
  • Islamabad: that income available in Portugal.
  • Lawyer practice: park 12 months of the floor in a Portuguese IBAN (~€11,040 single).
Safer Pakistani strategy

1) Recurring income 1.5×–2× the floor. 2) 6–12 months of statements with those credits. 3) Portuguese IBAN holding at least 12 months’ subsistence. 4) FBR returns that match. 5) Clean SWIFT trail. No cash stuffing.

Section 8

Income that counts — and income that does not

Usually accepted

  • Civil, military or private pension (Pakistan or foreign)
  • Rent you own, with registered leases + rent credits
  • Dividends from companies you do not run day-to-day
  • Interest, bond coupons, brokerage / fund distributions
  • Royalties (books, software, patents — scrutinised if “YouTube”)
  • Trust / annuity distributions

Not enough on their own

  • Salary → D8 or work visa
  • Freelance invoices / consulting → D8 or D2
  • One-time property sale with no yield
  • Family gifts, informal support, unexplained cash
  • Crypto profits with no tax history
  • “I will start earning after I arrive”
Savings-only files

Some consulates accept large savings as subsistence for residence visas in general. Islamabad’s D7 checklist is income-based. Relying on savings alone is risky here. Get a Portuguese lawyer to structure a savings-heavy case before filing.

The PKR problem

Pakistani rent in rupees is still income, but the officer thinks in euros. You must show conversion after bank charges still clears the floor, that SBP/bank compliance lets you transfer it, and that it is stable. If PKR falls between filing and AIMA, the file looks weaker at renewal. Hold part of the buffer in EUR or USD.

Active company owners

If you still manage a private limited company in Pakistan daily, officers may call it active income. Options: take dividends only and appoint a documented local manager, or file D2/D8 instead.

A

Decide and plan

Weeks 1–2 · steps 1–4
GateA written D7 / D8 / D2 verdict, a one-page income map and a dated plan.
1

Confirm D7 is your visa

Time 1–3 daysCost Free
  • List every income stream and tick it against the table in chapter 5.
  • If 70% or more is pension, rent, dividends, interest or royalties → D7. If 70% or more is salary or freelance → D8. If you are launching a business in Portugal → D2.
  • Never mix categories in a single file.
You end with: A written verdict: D7, D8 or D2.
Watch out

Salary and freelance invoices do not qualify for D7 on their own, however large. One-time property sales, family gifts and crypto profits with no tax history do not count either.

2

Write the one-page income map

Time 2–4 daysCost Free
  • One row per source: source and country · currency · 12-month average in EUR · tax paid (with your NTN) · how the money reaches Portugal (SWIFT route).
  • Total it against the floor for your household (chapter 4). Aim for 1.5×–2× the floor, not exactly €920.
You end with: Exhibit A — the backbone of your cover letter and financial pack.
3

Choose your team

Time About 1 weekCost Lawyer €1,500–€5,000+ (optional)
  • Decide whether to hire a Portuguese immigration lawyer. It is most useful when someone must act for you under power of attorney (NIF, bank, lease) or your case is not textbook.
  • Line up a Pakistani tax adviser now and a Portuguese contabilista for after you land.
You end with: A named lawyer / adviser and a signed engagement letter.
4

Set the calendar backwards from a filing date

Time 1 dayCost Free
  • Pick a target filing week and count back 10–12 weeks.
  • Order the PCC in the last 2–3 weeks. Civil-document apostilles must be under 6 months old on filing day.
  • Keep a versioned checklist with a “Known” versus “Needs verification” column.
You end with: A dated plan with every deadline written down.
Tip

Islamabad can take up to 15 working days just to answer your appointment email. Build that wait into the plan (see step 18).

Build your Portuguese foothold

Weeks 2–6 · steps 5–10
GateNIF in hand, Portuguese IBAN holding the buffer, and a registered 12-month lease or deed.
5

Prepare the power of attorney and ID pack

Time 1–2 weeksCost Notary + MOFA apostilleWhere Notary, then MOFA
  • Passport copy, proof of address, and a notarised, apostilled power of attorney naming your Portuguese representative or lawyer.
  • Powers of attorney usually need a personal visit to MOFA, so start your MOFA registration now (step 11).
You end with: Apostilled power of attorney plus a scanned ID set for your lawyer.
6

Get your NIF (Portuguese tax number)

Time 2–5 business days via a lawyer; allow up to 3 weeksCost €80–€300Where Remote, via a representante fiscal
  • Your representative applies at the Portuguese tax authority on your behalf.
  • Save the certificate PDF permanently — the NIF is valid for life. After you become resident, your contabilista converts it from non-resident to resident.
You end with: NIF certificate.
7

Open a Portuguese bank account

Time 1–2 weeksCost €0–€50 + €250–€500 opening depositWhere ActivoBank, Novobanco, Millennium BCP, Bison Bank
  • Open with your NIF, passport scan and proof of address. Some banks ask for extra KYC or a branch visit — your lawyer can often handle this.
  • Confirm the bank issues dated statements and note your IBAN.
You end with: Working IBAN and a first statement.
Watch out

Islamabad wants proof your income is “available in national territory”. A Portuguese account is how you show it.

8

Send a small test SWIFT transfer

Time 3–7 daysCost Bank charges
  • Send €100–€500 from your own Pakistani bank by SWIFT. Never hundi or hawala.
  • State the purpose as an own-account transfer or living expenses.
  • Keep the MT103, Form A and bank advice.
You end with: A clean transfer trail before you move the big amount.
9

Send the main buffer

Time 1–2 weeks (7–10 business days for large PKR conversions)Amount €11,040 single
  • Transfer 12 months of the floor: €11,040 for one adult, plus €5,520 per extra adult and €3,312 per child.
  • Consider holding part of it in EUR or USD to protect against a falling rupee between filing and AIMA.
  • Expect SBP and bank compliance questions; have your income map and statements ready. Keep every proof.
You end with: Buffer sitting in your Portuguese account plus the full proof pack.
Watch out

Cash stuffing and last-minute family transfers destroy credibility.

10

Secure a 12-month registered lease (or buy)

Time 2–4 weeksCost Deposit + 1–2 months, about €2,000–€5,000+Where Idealista, Imovirtual, Casa Sapo
  • Sign a lease of at least 12 months, in your name, with the landlord’s NIF, and register it on the Portal das Finanças. A purchase deed (any price) is also strong.
  • Portuguese landlords often demand 6–12 months’ rent upfront or a Portuguese guarantor (fiador) from foreign tenants. A lawyer acting under your power of attorney is the standard fix.
  • Airbnb, Booking.com and hotel reservations are rejected.
You end with: Registered lease (or deed) with proof of Finanças registration.
Watch out

Deposit scams are common. Never pay a landlord you have not verified.

14

Collect your financial evidence

Time 2–4 weeks
  • Stamped and signed bank statements for the last 6–12 months, from every account on your income map.
  • Income tax returns for the last 3 years and your FBR NTN certificate.
  • Pension letter (amount plus guarantee of receipt), registered rental agreements with rent-credit proofs, dividend or brokerage statements, and the property fard / registry if rent is your story.
You end with: Financial evidence pack.
15

Reconcile FBR, statements and income map

Time 2–3 days
  • The three must tell one story. If you declare PKR 2 million a year to FBR and €4,000 a month to Portugal, the file is toxic.
  • Annotate each recurring credit on your statements with a one-line note (source and purpose). Annotate real transactions; never alter them.
  • Fix mismatches with your tax adviser before filing.
You end with: A reconciled, annotated pack.
16

Buy travel medical insurance

Time 1 dayCost €80–€250
  • Minimum €30,000 cover (€50,000 is safer), valid in the Schengen Area, covering emergency medical treatment, urgent care and repatriation for the whole visa period (4 months).
  • Read the pre-existing-condition exclusions. Buy in the last 1–2 weeks before filing and ask the insurer about changing the start date.
You end with: Insurance certificate.
Watch out

Travel insurance will not satisfy AIMA. You will switch to a full-year private policy after arrival (step 29).

17

Order the Police Character Certificate — last

Time 5–15 working daysWhere Punjab Police Global Portal, Police Khidmat Markaz, local DPO, or ICT Police for Islamabad addresses
  • The PCC is valid for 90 days from issue and must still be under 90 days on appointment day. Do not start this clock until everything else is nearly ready.
  • Get it apostilled at MOFA immediately. Also obtain a certificate from any country where you lived 12 months or more.
  • Sign the Portuguese criminal-record consultation authorisation.
You end with: Apostilled PCC (clock now ticking).
Watch out

An out-of-date PCC on appointment day is one of the most common avoidable failures.

18

Email the Islamabad consular section for an appointment

Time Reply can take up to 15 working daysCost FreeWhere sconsular.islamabad@mne.pt
  • Send it the day you order the PCC so the appointment lands inside the PCC’s 90 days.
  • The body must contain: full name · passport number · occupation · travel plan · copies of previous visas · a detailed, self-explanatory purpose · signature block (full name, telephone, email).
  • Incomplete emails are disregarded. Do not pay third-party appointment agents.
You end with: Confirmed appointment date.

Get certified Portuguese translations

  • Urdu documents must be translated. Attach the translator’s credentials. English NADRA papers are often accepted at the visa centre, but AIMA and some officers prefer Portuguese.
You end with: Translated, apostilled document set.

Attend AIMA

Time 1 dayCost Official ~€150–€170; budget €185–€400 per person

Section 11

NIF, bank, housing

NIF — Número de Identificação Fiscal

Portuguese tax number. Needed to bank, lease, buy, and later file IRS. A non-EU person outside Portugal typically appoints a fiscal representative (representante fiscal). Remote cost about €80–€300. Via a lawyer acting under power of attorney, expect 2–5 business days; allow up to 3 weeks if you assemble the paperwork yourself. You will need a passport copy, proof of address and a notarised, apostilled power of attorney. You do not fly to Portugal just for a NIF. Keep the PDF forever. After you become resident, a contabilista should convert the NIF from non-resident to resident.

Portuguese bank

Islamabad’s “availability in national territory” is why this matters. Common 2026 non-resident options: Novobanco, ActivoBank, Millennium BCP and Bison Bank (often extra KYC or a branch visit). Initial deposit often €250–€500, plus the subsistence buffer.

Transfer sequence

Open account → SWIFT a test €100–€500 → then the main buffer (€11,040+ single, more for family) → keep every MT103, Form A, and bank advice. That pack is your source-of-funds exhibit.

Sending money from Pakistan

  • Bank SWIFT only. Not hundi / hawala.
  • Purpose wording the bank accepts: own-account transfer / living expenses / funds to own Portuguese account.
  • Expect SBP / compliance questions on source of funds.
  • Large PKR conversions: rate + 7–10 business days.
  • USD already in an FC or UAE/UK account is a cleaner trail.
FBR alignment

If you declare PKR 2 million a year to FBR and €4,000 a month to Portugal, the file is toxic.

Apostille — Pakistan is in the Hague system

Embassy of Portugal, Islamabad

Apostille must come from MOFA Pakistan. Civil docs generally < 6 months. PCC itself < 90 days. Portugal no longer legalises Pakistani public documents at the embassy — that is MOFA’s job.

From 27 July 2026 MOFA requires online pre-registration:

  1. Create an account at apostille.mofa.gov.pk
  2. Upload CNIC both sides and the documents
  3. Wait for email approval + challan
  4. Pay (online / Easypaisa / bank)

Section 14

Arrival and AIMA

At the border

Enter Portugal if you can (cleaner than first landing elsewhere). Carry passport + visa, insurance, housing, bank proof, printed lease, funds. Border police can still refuse entry.

Complete the AIMA residence appointment inside the 4-month visa window. If a date was printed when the visa issued, attend it.

AIMA — Agência para a Integração, Migrações e Asilo

Replaced SEF in 2023. Appointment: document check, photo, fingerprints, signature, fee, card posted to your Portuguese address.

Typical pack: passport + D7 visa (original and photocopy) · updated means · address proof: lease/deed + Atestado de Morada from the Junta de Freguesia · NIF · Portuguese bank statements · full-year private insurance or SNS · family proofs · signed criminal-record authorisation.

The banking litmus test

AIMA officers will ask for a freshly printed Portuguese bank statement showing the funds in a domestic account — at least 12× the minimum wage (about €11,040 single). Offshore wealth is irrelevant: the capital must physically be in Portugal before the appointment. Travel insurance will not satisfy AIMA; switch to a full-year policy once you land.

Fees: AIMA’s official analysis + issuance fee is about €150–€170. Practitioner and lawyer packages typically run €185–€400 per person. Confirm on aima.gov.pt.

Backlog

More than 400,000 cases were pending in early 2026. Appointment waits run from about 15 days (rural offices) to about 120 days (Lisbon / Porto). Some get a slot with the visa; others wait weeks to many months. Book as soon as you land — the deadline is 4 months from entry. If the 4-month visa will die before the slot, do not leave Schengen casually. Get written proof of a pending appointment. Lawyers sometimes file an ação de intimação to force a date — last resort. Until the card arrives you have only 2 visa entries. Avoid extra foreign trips.

First card: 2 years. Renewal: 3 years if income, housing, insurance/SNS and stay rules still hold.

Section 16

Application-building methods

A complete file is not a guaranteed approval, but incomplete files are increasingly rejected. These eight methods turn a pile of papers into a case an officer can follow in ten minutes.

1 · The income map

A one-page table of every income source: country, currency, 12-month average in EUR, tax paid, and route to Portugal. It becomes Exhibit A in your cover letter.

2 · Bank-statement narrative

Classify and annotate transactions so recurring credits over 6–12 months clearly match the income map. Personal transfers are never “income”. Annotate real activity; never edit or stage it.

3 · FBR alignment

Your Pakistani returns must match what you declare to Portugal. Align both sides before filing, not after a question arrives.

4 · Cover letter as case file

One to two pages: who you are, your income, why Portugal, housing, family, intent to reside. Reference every attached document.

5 · Document versioning

Use a versioned checklist with a “Known” versus “Needs verification” filter. Re-download the Islamabad D7 PDF the week you file.

6 · Legal proxy for lease and bank

A Portuguese lawyer under power of attorney can secure the 12-month registered lease, open the bank account and coordinate Finanças registration — solving the “cannot rent from abroad” catch-22.

7 · The buffer strategy

Park 12 months of the required income in a Portuguese IBAN (€11,040 single). Hold part in EUR or USD to protect against PKR depreciation between filing and AIMA.

8 · Pre-emptive PCC timing

Order the PCC last: it lives 90 days. NADRA papers, apostilles and translations should all be finished before that clock starts.

Section 19

Tax in 2026 — the blindside

D7 stay rules push you over 183 days. Plan on becoming a Portuguese tax resident on worldwide income.

NHR is gone for new people

The old non-habitual resident regime closed. Transition ended 31 March 2025. Replacement IFICI / “NHR 2.0” is a narrow science/innovation incentive. Ordinary D7 pensioners and landlords do not qualify. There is no “Portugal does not tax foreign income” rule for new D7 residents in 2026.

IRS is progressive (bands commonly quoted ~13%–48% plus possible municipal surcharge). Investment income often ~28% flat unless aggregated. Foreign rent, dividends, interest and pensions may be taxable in Portugal, with a credit for tax already paid in Pakistan under the Pakistan–Portugal Double Tax Convention (signed 23 June 2000, in force via SRO 734(I)/2007). Apply it with a tax lawyer — do not DIY treaty articles.

Moving does not end FBR by magic. You may need Pakistani non-resident status, a leaving/tax-file update, and continued tax on Pakistan-source rent/dividends as a non-resident. Get a Pakistani adviser and a Portuguese contabilista before you fly.

Year-1 tax to-dos

  • NIF resident conversion · Portal das Finanças
  • Annual IRS return
  • Local work → Segurança Social
  • IMI if you buy property
  • Foreign-account reporting as required
Section 20

Stay rules, renewal, PR, citizenship

Minimum stay

Do not stay outside Portugal more than 6 consecutive months, or 8 months in total, during the permit. For a 2-year first card this is often summarised as about 16 months in Portugal in the first 2 years. Golden-Visa “7 days a year” does not apply.

Renewal at year 2

Housing, means, insurance/SNS, tax number/compliance, actual residence, clean record → 3-year card.

Permanent residence — still 5 years

A2 Portuguese, means, housing, no serious record, tax/social-security compliance. PR is not citizenship. The 2026 nationality reform did not abolish 5-year PR.

Citizenship — the May 2026 change

NationalityLegal residence for naturalisation
EU or CPLP (Brazil, Angola, Mozambique, Cabo Verde…)7 years
Everyone else, including Pakistan10 years

Clock starts at issuance of the residence title, not the visa application. Also expected: A2 CIPLE, civic knowledge, democratic-rule-of-law declaration, tightened criminal thresholds. New applicants in September 2026 should plan on 10 years. Portuguese citizenship is EU citizenship — now a decade project, not a five-year project.

Dual nationality

Portugal allows dual citizenship. Pakistan allows it only with a notified list (~22 countries in 2026: US, UK, Canada, Australia, NZ, Germany, France, Italy, Belgium, Switzerland, Netherlands, Sweden, Norway, Denmark, Finland, Ireland, Iceland, Luxembourg, Bahrain, Egypt, Jordan, Syria). Portugal is not on that list. A residence card does not affect Pakistani citizenship. Naturalising as Portuguese may put Pakistani nationality at risk. Confirm in writing with DGIP / Interior / a nationality lawyer before you ever file for a Portuguese passport.

Section 28

Honest assessment

D7 is real and open to Pakistanis. There is no nationality ban. The legal bar is low compared with US/UK immigration.

The hard parts are not the €920. They are clean transferable income, parking funds in Portugal, apostille timing, actually living there, tax on worldwide income without NHR, a 10-year citizenship clock, and possible loss of Pakistani citizenship if you naturalise.

Good candidates

Retired officer with pension + rent and euro savings · tax-paid landlords who want EU schools · brokerage investors already filing taxes · Pakistanis already abroad with documented passive income, filing in the country of residence.

Weak candidates

Salary earner hoping D7 is easier than a work visa · someone whose brother will “show his account” · crypto/cash business with no NTN · a person who wants a Schengen travel document but will stay in Pakistan.
