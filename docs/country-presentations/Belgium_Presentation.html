Country Snapshot

Pakistan-Specific Fee Advantage
French Community: Pakistan is an exempt country (ARES Circular No. 2025-001)
Pakistani nationals at French Community institutions (UCLouvain, ULB, Liège, Namur, Saint-Louis, ICHEC, EPHEC, HEPL) may qualify for the standard EU-equivalent tuition (~EUR 835-1,194/year) instead of the non-EU surcharged rate (~EUR 5,010-5,369/year) -- "Pakistan listed as exempt country under certain conditions" per cross-country eligibility research. Always confirm current exemption status with the institution before quoting -- circulars can change year to year.
Tuition -- Flemish Community (Non-EU)
EUR 2,300 - 9,500/year
KU Leuven, Ghent, Antwerp, VUB, Hasselt, and the Flemish university colleges -- no broad exemption for non-EU students.
Tuition -- French Community (Non-EU)
EUR 5,010 - 5,369/year
UCLouvain, ULB, Liège, Mons, Namur, Saint-Louis, and the French Community Hautes Écoles -- but see the Pakistan exemption note above.
Institutions Covered
20 shown (19 fully profiled)
11 universities + 8 university colleges with confirmed English-taught programmes, plus the German-speaking Community’s sole institution (confirmed to have none).
Language Communities
Flemish, French, German-speaking
Belgium’s higher-education system is organised by community, each with its own funding and admission rules -- always check which community an institution belongs to (see each profile in Section 3).
Visa Timeline (Pakistan)
Varies by institution: 3 weeks - 4 months
University of Geneva-style fast processing does not apply here; Ghent University is the slowest documented (3-4 months official) -- see Section 9 for the full range.
English-Taught Depth
Master’s-heavy; limited at Bachelor’s
KU Leuven alone offers 62 fully English-taught Master’s programmes; most universities' Bachelor’s remain predominantly Dutch/French-taught, with a handful of English tracks.
Health Insurance, PR Pathway, Citizenship Pathway, Dual Nationality, Embassy Warnings, National Application Portal, Study Gap & Age Policy
Information required for all of these — none were confirmed in this research pass. Do not state a position on any of these to a client without checking a current, authoritative source first. There is no single national application portal — each institution runs its own admissions portal, listed individually in each institution’s profile in Section 3.

Visa & Post-Study Work

Pakistan-Specific Requirements
• French Community fee exemption: Pakistan is listed as an exempt country under certain conditions (ARES Circular No. 2025-001) — may reduce tuition from ~EUR 5,010-5,369/year to the EU-equivalent ~EUR 835-1,194/year at UCLouvain, ULB, Liège, Mons, Namur, Saint-Louis, ICHEC, EPHEC, and HEPL. Confirm current status before quoting.
• Howest University of Applied Sciences: confirmed early deadline of 1 May 2026 for Pakistani students — flag clearly to any client interested in Howest.
• ULB: requires a 13/20 average in secondary school plus equivalence to the Belgian CESS certificate.
• University of Mons: requires a 13/20 average overall and 12/20 in field-specific subjects.
• English requirement: IELTS 6.5+ / TOEFL iBT 90+ typical; KU Leuven requires IELTS 7.0 for some programmes.
Processing Timelines
Official processing times vary widely by institution: KU Leuven 6-8 weeks, Ghent University 3-4 months (the slowest documented in this dataset), Vrije Universiteit Brussel up to 10 weeks. Other institutions rely on unofficial student-forum estimates of 4-6 weeks — treat those as estimates only. Direct enquiry to admissions offices remains advisable for a definitive timeline on any specific case.
Counselor Note
Belgium’s French-Community fee exemption for Pakistani nationals is the single biggest cost lever in this deck — a client choosing between a Flemish and a French-Community institution of similar quality could see their annual tuition drop by roughly EUR 4,000–4,500 if the exemption applies. Always confirm current exemption status directly with the institution’s admissions office, since circulars can change year to year.
