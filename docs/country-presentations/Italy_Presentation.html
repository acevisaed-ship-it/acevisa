Italy at a Glance

Critical Update — IMAT Abolished
Public medical schools no longer use the IMAT entrance test
Replaced by the "Open Semester" (Semestre Aperto) system — confirmed extended for 2026/27 via Ministerial Decree No. 941 (10 July 2026). Students enrol without an admission test, complete a common first semester, and progression to the next semester is decided by first-semester exam results and national ranking. 52,000 students competed for 18,889 places in 2026/27. Private medical schools (Humanitas, San Raffaele, UniCamillus, Cattolica) still use their own selection tests (HUMAT, UniSR Test, etc.) — IMAT abolition applies to public universities only.

Visa Process & Application Timing

Visa Document Checklist (Embassy Islamabad)
• National Visa (D) form, ICAO photo, valid passport (3+ months beyond visa)
• Health insurance — minimum €30,000 Schengen coverage
• Pre-enrolment via the Universitaly portal, validated by the university
• CIMEA Certificate (Comparability + Verification) OR Declaration of Value
• Educational documents — apostilled + translated into Italian
• B2 language certificate (Italian or English programme)
• Proof of funds: €10,179.85/year; bank statements — last 12 months; income tax returns — last 3 years
• Apostilled Family Registration Certificate (FRC)
Attestation & Process
• IBCC, HEC, and MoFA attestation required before visa submission
• Documentation valid 6 months from date of issue
• Universitaly validation gates the Embassy appointment — booked via BLS-Intiana in validation-date order
• Fraud warning: Embassy Islamabad reports a rise in fraudulent Pakistani documents for 2026/27 — financial documents are being scrutinised more rigorously than before
Scholarships Worth Flagging
• DSU regional scholarships: full tuition + stipend + accommodation — University of Milan (deadline 30 Sep 2026, strictly no extension), DSU Toscana/Pisa (7 Sep 2026) — early and unforgiving deadlines
• MAECI scholarships: Ministry of Foreign Affairs award for Pakistani applicants under 28 with a relevant bachelor's
• LUISS: 30 international Master's scholarships (20 full + 10 at 50%) · Bocconi: merit-based full/partial waivers · Cattolica: tuition reduced to €5,900–6,400 · Humanitas: €5,000–23,000/year merit awards

Quote Carefully — Corrections & Safeguards

Counselor Notes — Verified Source, Still Confirm Before Quoting
• This deck is built from a cross-checked, verified research report (MUR circulars, Embassy Islamabad, university pages, WHO Observatory) — a meaningful upgrade over the earlier Italy deck, which carried heavy data-reliability warnings from an unverified source.
• The IMAT-abolition finding is the single most important update — the previous deck described IMAT as active; it is not, for public universities, as of the Ministerial Decree extending the Open Semester system through 2026/27.
• Public-university tuition is income-based (ISEE) — the €900–4,000 range is real but the exact figure a given family pays depends on declared income; don't quote a single flat number as guaranteed.
• 74 institutions in this deck are registry-only (name/city, no individually confirmed programme list or fee) — present them as "Italy also has..." breadth, not as fully researched options.
• Course lists on the following pages are the confirmed English-taught programmes found in the source research, not guaranteed-exhaustive catalogues — always confirm the live list on the university's own site before quoting a client.
• DSU scholarship deadlines are early (Sept) and explicitly not extended in past cycles — flag this well ahead of the application season, not as an afterthought.
• Financial-document scrutiny has increased for Pakistani applicants specifically — prepare clients for a more rigorous review than in prior cycles.
