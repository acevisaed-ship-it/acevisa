Section 1

Country Snapshot

Pakistan-Specific Scholarship Advantage
Korean Government Scholarship Program (GKS) is confirmed available to Pakistani applicants
Age caps apply: Master's under 30, PhD under 35, Research (non-degree) under 50. GKS is a genuine national scholarship route (unlike most countries in this project, where none was confirmed) -- see Section 8A for detail. Always confirm current GKS cycle dates and quota directly before advising a client.
Tuition (Non-Korean)
KRW 3M - 8M/semester
National/public range KRW 3,000,000-6,000,000; private range KRW 5,000,000-8,000,000/semester. KAIST is a notable exception -- full tuition waiver for students maintaining GPA above 2.0/4.3.
Application Windows
Spring: Sep-Oct · Fall: Mar-Apr
Exact dates vary sharply by institution -- see each institution's own profile in Section 3 and the consolidated table in Section 9.
Institutions Covered
26 shown (5 fully profiled)
Source itself scopes to "26 major institutions -- a representative sample of the ~200+ four-year institutions in South Korea" -- an explicit, deliberate narrowing, not a hidden gap. Of these 26, the source's own Data Quality Report confirms full individual research for 5; the remaining 21 appear here as registry-only entries rather than omitted.
Language Requirement
TOPIK 3+ or IELTS 5.5+/TOEFL 71+
Korean-taught track needs TOPIK (Level 5 for Korean Language and Literature/Education; Level 4 for International Trade; Level 2 for arts/PE); English track needs IELTS/TOEFL/CEFR B2 -- SNU/Yonsei/KAIST specifically want IELTS 6.5+/TOEFL iBT 83-90+.
Documents
Apostille required
All educational documents need Apostille -- budget extra processing time for this step specifically before submission.
"SKY" Universities
Seoul National, Korea, Yonsei
Colloquially Korea's most prestigious university trio -- all three are fully profiled in Section 3, with KAIST/SKKU as the two other fully profiled institutions.
Health Insurance, PR Pathway, Citizenship Pathway, Dual Nationality, Embassy Warnings, National Application Portal, Study Gap & Age Policy (Degree Programmes), Turnaround Time
Information required for all of these — none were confirmed in this research pass (turnaround time is separately flagged "Not yet extracted" in the source's own Data Quality Report). Do not state a position on any of these to a client without checking a current, authoritative source first. There is no single national application portal — each institution runs its own admissions portal, listed individually in each institution's profile in Section 3.
