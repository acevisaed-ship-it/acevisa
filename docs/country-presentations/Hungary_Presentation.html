Section 7

Visa & Post-Study Work

Hungarian Student Residence Permit — General Process (non-EU / Pakistani applicants)
Pakistani nationals apply in person for a type D student residence permit at the nearest Hungarian Consulate (Islamabad). After the visa interview, processing commonly takes 4–5 weeks — book the interview as early as possible. Once granted, the type D visa allows a single entry to Hungary; you then have 30 days after arrival to report to the Immigration Office in Budapest and receive your residence permit card.
Typical Requirements
Original school/ID documents • institution's visa-support / admission certificate confirming student status, accommodation and health insurance • proof of financial means from a regular-income account (IBS's own guidance cites roughly EUR 8,000) • proof of accommodation • health insurance (paid on arrival at some institutions — e.g. IBS: ~HUF 120,000 / ~EUR 300) • English-language certificate • basic knowledge of Hungary and the chosen programme (may be tested at the visa interview)

Section 12A

Quote Carefully — Country-Wide Safeguards

Country-Wide Safeguards
• This deck covers only 2 of Hungary's 40+ higher-education institutions — present these as strong starting options, not the full extent of what Hungary offers.
• Visa financial-requirement figures differ by source: IBS's own visa guidance cites ~EUR 8,000 as one year's proof-of-funds, while the separate practical-info research pass cites ~HUF 200,000/month (~EUR 500, ~EUR 6,000/year). These are not necessarily contradictory (a consulate can want a lump-sum balance larger than the monthly minimum it checks against) but come from two different sources — verify the current combined requirement with the consulate or institution before quoting a client.
• PR pathway, citizenship pathway, dual-nationality status with Pakistan, visa fee, and embassy fraud warnings were not confirmed in any source reviewed — do not state a position on any of these without checking a current, authoritative source first.
